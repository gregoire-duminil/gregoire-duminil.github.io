About

At BNP Paribas I lead hedge fund due diligence, write risk memos, and take limit recommendations to credit committees. I also monitor exposures, handle multi-asset initial margins, and work through ISDA/CSA, GMRA/MRA, and PBA negotiations.

That sits on a full path through the bank: I spent nearly two years as a P&L controller on futures, options, equities, swaps, and structured products before moving into credit risk. Earlier roles include client services at ProRealTime, ESG research for a French Ministry of Finance mandate at Roche-Brune Asset Management, and stock loan operations at Natixis Asset Management.

I manage a multi-asset personal portfolio (equities, ETFs, options, futures) and write Python tools for backtesting and option analysis when something on my desk or in the market needs it. Multi-cultural background, I both speak and write French, English and Spanish. CFA candidate.
